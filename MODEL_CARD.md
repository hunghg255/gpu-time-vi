# gpu-time-vi model card

## Model

The published package embeds the checkpoint named in `packages/training/active/export-report.json`, which is the only place to read the artifact hash and every score from. Never quote a score from this file or from memory.

The model has 38,745 parameters, two scan layers, 580 embedding rows, and 36 role labels over 40 slots: gpu-time's 35 roles plus `LUNAR` for the lunar-calendar markers `âm lịch`, `ÂL`, `mùng`, `rằm` and `nhuận`. A 40x40 CRF transition matrix supports Viterbi decoding. Weights use 6-bit symmetric per-tensor quantization with f32 intermediates. The full published package must stay under the 50,000-byte Brotli release limit; `packages/benchmark/results/size.json` records the current measurement.

The model predicts one role per token and a boundary score that splits the input into expressions; `calibrate.py` fits the threshold on the development splits. Timezone, the lunar calendar and calendar arithmetic are not model roles: TypeScript resolves them after the model runs.

## Intended use

The model turns short Vietnamese time expressions — reminders, chat messages, schedule forms, notes — into dates, ranges and recurrence rules in the browser. It reads the solar and the Vietnamese lunar calendar.

It is not suitable for parsing long documents, for legal, medical, billing or compliance scheduling, or for any decision where a wrong date has real consequences. It supports Vietnamese **with tone marks** only; unaccented text (`ngay mai`), English sentences, sexagenary years (`năm Bính Ngọ`) and earthly-branch hours (`giờ Tý`) are out of scope and can return a wrong result with no diagnostic.

## Training data

All supervision is generated by this repository's own renderers, never by the runtime parser and never by an external teacher — chrono-node and Microsoft Recognizers have no Vietnamese locale, so the harvested "real" corpus of gpu-time has no counterpart here.

- `torch/semantic.py` samples a schedule and renders it (about 55% of rows). Every rendered span round-trips through the real compiler.
- `torch/natural.py` wraps the renderers in ten families of sentence frames (about 25%). Five frames are reserved and never trained on.
- `torch/background.py` writes negatives (about 20%), forty percent of them hard negatives built on ambiguous syllables: `năm người`, `chiều cao`, `tối đa`, `sáng tạo`, `hoa mai`, `đường 3/2`, phone numbers, prices, scores.
- Background prose around expressions comes from the Tatoeba `vie` export (11,050 sentences after filtering out every time word, spoken number and time-shaped digit; pinned in `data/corpus.json`).

The shipped model trains on 60,000 fresh rows per epoch. Generated scores share rendering families with training and measure coverage of the generators, not accuracy on real user language.

## Evaluation

Read the numbers from `packages/training/active/export-report.json` (`metrics`, `promotion`) and `packages/benchmark/results/REPORT.md`. The corpora are:

- Gold `grammar` (300 authored surface forms, 35 families), `prose` (45), `adversarial` (28) and `negatives` (45), exact schedule through the built package.
- Gold `results` (61 resolved date expectations, including three DST cases in `Europe/Berlin`) through the public API.
- Generated reserved carriers and bare expressions, which gate promotion.
- Token and exact-sequence accuracy on validation and held-out splits.

Parity is a separate gate: 512 fixtures compare decoded int6 inference against PyTorch logits, and `pnpm test:browser` compares 10,000 sequences in real WebGPU on Chrome.

## Limitations

- Tone marks are required. The tokenizer keeps `sáu` and `sau` apart through the word hash; without marks they are the same word.
- `sau` and `trước` are read as shifts before a quantity and as modifiers after a unit; sentences that break that order can flip meaning.
- `cuối tuần` alone is one weekend; the series needs `mỗi`/`hàng`. `tháng giêng` and `tháng chạp` are lunar; `tháng 1` and `tháng 12` are solar. `mùng X hàng tháng` repeats on the solar calendar with a `lunar-ignored` warning.
- A bare weekday resolves to the next such day, `thứ ba tuần trước` to the Tuesday of the previous calendar week (Monday-first), and a clock alone that has passed today to tomorrow, following gpu-time's conventions.
- Lunar conversion follows the UTC+7 rule of the official Vietnamese calendar; Tết 2030 is 2 February in Vietnam and 3 February in China.
- The middle of a year (`giữa năm`) has no fixed date and returns `unsupported-edge`.
- Ambiguous numeric dates read day first (`3/4` is 3 April) unless the caller sets `dateOrder: "MDY"`.
- Accuracy on real user phrasing is unmeasured; report failures into `packages/training/data/gold/user-failures.jsonl`.
